# Lineup Lab Support

Lineup Lab is a fantasy football lab (start/sit calls, waiver pickups, trades and projections built on real NFL data)
and the home of **The Daily Drive**, a free daily NFL trivia game.

## Contact

Email **lineuplabfootball@gmail.com**. We read every message and usually reply within a day or two.

## Common questions

**How do I connect my league?** Open the app, sign in, and add your league from My Team. Sleeper leagues need only the
league link or ID. ESPN leagues need to be public, or you can paste your ESPN cookies (see the in-app note).

**The app says my lineup data is stale.** NFL data refreshes daily; live game scores refresh during games. Pull down or
switch tabs to reload.

**How does The Daily Drive work?** Seven prompts a day. Name a player who fits; the rarer your answer, the more yards
you gain. A new drive starts every day at midnight US Eastern.

**How do I turn off notifications?** iPhone or iPad Settings → Notifications → Lineup Lab, or More → Settings in the app.

**How do I delete my account?** In the app: More → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and everything
tied to it (connected leagues, settings, scores, notification tokens). On the web: Settings → Delete account.
