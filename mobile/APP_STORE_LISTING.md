**Description:**
Lineup Lab is a fantasy football decision tool that turns real NFL data into clear answers.

• Player values and projections built from play-by-play and Next Gen Stats, not guesswork
• Start/Sit help for your actual lineup, with matchup and recent-form context
• Trade Finder and trade grades that account for your roster needs
• Waiver wire pickups ranked by how much they help YOUR team
• Injury-aware numbers that factor in the player's status
• Live draft board for Sleeper snake drafts
• Connect Sleeper leagues (and ESPN leagues, beta) and switch between teams
• Your account works on the website and the app

Lineup Lab is a decision-support tool, not betting or financial advice. It is not affiliated with the NFL, any team, Sleeper, or ESPN.
