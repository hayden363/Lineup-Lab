Your ESPN league must be set to public. This app can only read a league that anyone with the link can view. To turn that on: open your league on ESPN, go to League → Settings → Basic Settings (commissioner only), and tick “Make League Viewable to Public”. Then paste the league URL or ID here. Not the commissioner? Ask them to switch it on. You can switch it back off after connecting.
This isn't an official ESPN integration — it uses your own ESPN session cookies to read your league the same way your browser does, and may stop working if ESPN changes something on their end. About Lineup Lab

A from-scratch fantasy valuation engine. Every number here is computed from free, public NFL data — nothing scraped from a paid service.

  • Base metrics — efficiency & volume stats from real weekly box scores (nflverse).
  • Advanced metrics — real Next Gen Stats tracking data: separation, cushion, CPOE, rush yards over expected. The free, legal equivalent of what a paid grading service like PFF sells.
  • PVS (Player Value Score) — 0–100: how much more a player gives your lineup than a freely available replacement at his position, backed by usage, ceiling and stability. Comparable across QB, RB, WR and TE.
  • Matchups — a real, multi-stat defensive profile (run defense, pass defense, pressure — many categories, not one blanket number), matched to the specific metrics each position's SCORE is built from.
  • My Team, Start/Sit, Trade, Waivers, News — decision tools built on the same board, synced to your real Sleeper roster, with real trending-add data from Sleeper on the waiver wire.
  • Multiple teams — connect every league you play in and switch which one is "active" any time in Settings above.

No PFF data is used anywhere in this app. Account emails (verification, password reset) go out via a Gmail account the server operator configures — see Settings → Account for this account's verification status. ESPN cookies are encrypted at rest and never shown back to you in full.
